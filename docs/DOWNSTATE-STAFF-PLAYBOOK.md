# Downstate Lead Handling Playbook

This is the office workflow for Friendly Party Rental leads coming from the Riverdale / Bronx / Lower Westchester Downstate site.

## 1. Identify the lead

Downstate website inquiries are saved in Friendly Party Rental's existing Planning Inquiries system.

Look for:

- green **Downstate** badge;
- message tag `[DOWNSTATE RENTAL INQUIRY]`;
- event address and city;
- property / venue type;
- setup surface;
- usable dimensions, if supplied;
- access / site notes;
- requested rentals;
- original landing/source information.

Office notification emails use **[DOWNSTATE RENTAL]** in the subject.

The admin source filter can be set to **Downstate**.

## 2. First review — qualify before calling it a real opportunity

Check:

1. **Date** — Is the date potentially serviceable?
2. **Address** — Is it in Riverdale, selected Bronx territory, Yonkers, Mount Vernon, New Rochelle, or another accepted lower-Westchester area?
3. **Order size** — Does the request plausibly support the Downstate complete-event minimum?
4. **Equipment** — Is the request based on inventory Friendly actually carries?
5. **Site** — Does the described property appear potentially suitable?
6. **Access** — Any gate, stairs, long carry, parking/loading, elevator, or dense-city issue?
7. **Surface** — Grass, pavement, patio, mixed, indoor?
8. **Event timing** — Any hard exact setup or pickup requirement?
9. **Venue rules** — Insurance, COI, vendor approval, park/school requirements, or stake restrictions?

Do not quote only from guest count.

## 3. Minimum-order rule

Current Downstate public positioning:

> Complete-event deliveries generally start around $1,500 before tax.

This is a qualification floor, not a universal fixed package price.

The actual quote must consider:

- rental equipment;
- setup and breakdown labor;
- truck/route time;
- tolls and parking;
- long carry / difficult access;
- tent anchoring or weights;
- exact-time requirements;
- pickup timing;
- venue or documentation requirements;
- additional crew;
- taxes and applicable fees.

Do not copy Syracuse delivery pricing into a Downstate quote.

## 4. When to ask for site photos

Request photos before finalizing a tent or inflatable quote when the setup is not obvious.

Ask for:

- one wide photo of the full setup area;
- gate / delivery path;
- driveway or paved area;
- slope or grade;
- nearby fences, pools, buildings, trees, and sheds;
- overhead wires / limbs;
- stairs, elevators, or narrow passages;
- venue loading area when relevant.

For tents, also request approximate usable dimensions.

For inflatables, confirm access width, usable flat area, power, and water when needed.

## 5. Tent decision

### Pole tent may fit when

- suitable grass site;
- staking is allowed;
- working clearance exists;
- underground/overhead conflicts are addressed.

### Frame / weighted setup may be needed when

- asphalt or concrete;
- staking is restricted;
- tight site;
- venue requires a non-staked solution.

Never promise a paved-site setup until the equipment and anchoring/weight plan are confirmed.

## 6. Inflatable decision

Confirm:

- exact unit;
- setup dimensions;
- flat usable surface;
- clear delivery path;
- gate width;
- approved anchoring;
- power;
- water for wet units;
- overhead clearance;
- venue/property rules.

Do not assume every Bronx property can accept an inflatable.

## 7. Quote-building order

Build the quote in this order:

1. Core tent / inflatable / event structure.
2. Required seating and tables.
3. Linens / covers.
4. Lighting, fans, generator/power.
5. Dance floor / cocktail tables.
6. Games, concessions, photo booth, coolers, accessories.
7. Delivery/setup/pickup logistics.
8. Site-specific labor or equipment.
9. Taxes / final charges.

The customer should be able to see why the total is different from a Syracuse pickup or local delivery order.

## 8. Suggested first response

Use this structure:

> Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out to Friendly Party Rental about your [date] event in [area]. I have your request for [main items]. Before I finalize the Downstate quote, I just need to confirm [missing site/access details]. Once I have that, I can confirm the equipment, delivery/setup plan, and final pricing.

If photos are needed:

> Please reply with a wide photo of the setup area and the access path/gate. If you are requesting a tent, approximate usable dimensions are also helpful.

## 9. When to decline or hold

Do not force a quote when:

- order cannot support route economics;
- site is clearly unsuitable;
- requested setup conflicts with equipment limitations;
- address is outside accepted service territory;
- venue rules cannot be met;
- date/inventory is unavailable;
- customer will not provide required site/access information.

A clean decline is better than accepting a job that creates a loss or unsafe setup.

## 10. Track the outcome

For the 90-day test, record:

- area;
- event date;
- lead source;
- main requested category;
- qualified / not qualified;
- quote amount;
- booked / lost;
- lost reason;
- estimated route/labor problem if applicable;
- equipment requested that Friendly did not have;
- whether Syracuse distance was the main reason the job was lost.

The Downstate decision should be based on profitable booked work, not raw inquiry count.


## Lead stages

Use the Downstate stage controls in **Admin → Planning inquiries**:

- **New** — inquiry received, not yet qualified.
- **Qualified** — date, territory, order size, and site appear worth pursuing.
- **Quoted** — a real quote/order record has been created from the inquiry.
- **Booked** — the linked order has become Active.
- **Lost** — not moving forward; record a concise lost reason.

Creating a quote with **Create quote from this lead** automatically links the order and marks the lead **Quoted**.
When that linked order becomes **Active**, the lead automatically becomes **Booked**.

## Create Quote handoff

For a Downstate inquiry, use **Create quote from this lead** instead of retyping the customer manually.

The order builder preloads:
- customer name;
- email;
- phone;
- event date;
- event address;
- city;
- ZIP;
- customer-submitted event/site context in internal notes.

Staff must still choose:
- actual inventory items;
- quantities;
- current unit prices;
- schedule;
- travel/delivery fee;
- setup surface;
- any exact-time fee;
- taxes, discounts, and final quote details.

Do not turn the customer’s free-text requested-rentals sentence directly into priced line items without verifying the actual catalog item.


## Downstate route fee review

When a Downstate inquiry is opened in the normal Friendly order builder:

- the ordinary Syracuse/Minoa ZIP-based travel-fee calculator is disabled for that inquiry;
- staff must enter the Downstate route/delivery fee manually;
- staff must check the **Downstate route review** acknowledgment before creating the quote;
- the reviewed fee is written into the order's internal notes.

Review at minimum:

- round-trip truck time;
- crew hours;
- fuel;
- tolls;
- parking/loading restrictions;
- setup and breakdown labor;
- pickup timing / second-trip risk;
- special access or long carry;
- whether the job still clears the Downstate minimum and target contribution.

Do not click a Syracuse auto-fee button or copy a local delivery fee into a Downstate quote.
