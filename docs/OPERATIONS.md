# Downstate Operations Rules

## Territory strategy

Core test zone:
- Riverdale
- Fieldston
- Kingsbridge
- Yonkers
- Mount Vernon
- New Rochelle
- Lower Westchester

Secondary:
- selected Bronx jobs that fit equipment, site, route, and minimum-order economics

Manhattan:
- selective premium jobs only during the first test

## Site suitability

Before confirming a job, capture:
- exact event address
- surface type: grass / asphalt / concrete / mixed
- approximate usable dimensions
- access width and stairs/gates
- parking/loading restrictions
- overhead obstructions
- underground utility concerns for staked tents
- power/water needs for inflatables or lighting
- venue/school/property insurance requirements

## Equipment fit

Use pole tents only on sites suitable for staking and required clearances.
Use frame/weighted options for paved or stake-restricted locations when the equipment and engineering requirements allow it.
Inflatables require adequate flat space, anchoring, power, supervision, weather limits, and any required site approvals.

## Route economics

Every Downstate quote should account for:
- round-trip vehicle time
- staff hours
- fuel
- tolls
- parking/loading
- setup complexity
- pickup timing
- second-trip risk
- equipment utilization opportunity cost

Do not apply Syracuse delivery pricing automatically.

## Warehouse trigger

Do not lease a Downstate warehouse because leads exist.
Consider local space when booked profitable work is repeatedly constrained by Syracuse travel, staging, or return-trip costs.

Initial warehouse search geography if the test succeeds:
- Yonkers
- Mount Vernon
- nearby lower Westchester industrial areas

The goal is access to Riverdale + Bronx + Westchester without paying Manhattan real-estate economics.
