## Paid search principle

Start narrow. The goal is to learn which neighborhoods and rental categories produce profitable leads, not to maximize clicks.

Recommended campaign splits:
- Riverdale / Fieldston / Kingsbridge
- Yonkers / Mount Vernon
- New Rochelle / Lower Westchester
- premium tent + wedding intent
- inflatable / family-party intent

Use negative keywords aggressively for:
- purchase / for sale
- DIY tent
- jobs / employment
- free
- indoor play centers
- tiny single-item orders if they cannot meet route economics

## Conversion tracking

Track:
- quote form submit
- call click
- booking / deposit when possible
- source campaign
- neighborhood / city
- requested category
- estimated quote value
- booked value

Do not optimize the campaign only for cheap leads. Optimize toward profitable booked events once enough data exists.
